This is sample text for a demonstration site. A real clinic would have it checked by a lawyer.

## Our aim

We want everyone to be able to use this website. We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 AA. You can use the site with a keyboard, a screen reader and large text.

## Our building

We have a step-free entrance and an accessible toilet. See the [contact page](/contact/) for more.

## Tell us if something is hard to use

Call us or use the [contact page](/contact/). We will help you ask for an appointment another way.

<div data-market-only="CA">

## Ontario

We follow the Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act (AODA). If you need information in another format, tell us and we will provide it.
