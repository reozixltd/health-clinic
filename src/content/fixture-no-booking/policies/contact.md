## Getting here

<div data-market-only="AU">

We are a short walk from the tram stop on Example Street. There is two-hour street parking out the front and a small car park behind the building. Ask us at booking if you would like a time when parking is easier.

</div>

## Getting in

There is a step-free entrance, a lift to every floor and an accessible toilet. Assistance animals are welcome. If you would like someone to meet you at the door, tell us when you book. Read our [accessibility statement](/accessibility/) for more.

## After hours

<div data-market-only="AU">

We are closed outside opening hours. Leave a message and we reply on the next working day. For an emergency, call 000.

</div>
