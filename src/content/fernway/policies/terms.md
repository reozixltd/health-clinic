## Using this website

This website helps you ask for an appointment and find our details. It does not give medical advice. Do not use it in an emergency.

## Cancelling or moving a visit

Please tell us at least one business day before your visit. We may charge a part of the fee if you miss a visit without telling us. Call us on the number on our [contact page](/contact/) to change a time.

## Fees

Our [fees list](/fees/) shows our current fees. We tell you the fee before you book. If a fee changes, we tell you first.
