Hannah works at a calm pace. In the first session she mostly listens and asks gentle questions. Together you agree on what you would like to work on, and how often you would like to meet. There is no pressure to share more than you want to.

She has a special interest in anxiety, low mood and the kind of stress that builds up slowly. Her sessions often include practical skills you can try between visits, but nothing is ever required of you.

Outside the clinic, Hannah goes to a weekly pottery class and walks her dog along the river on weekends. She speaks English and Spanish, and likes a good bookshop.
