Elena begins with a conversation, not a mirror. She asks how your teeth have been, what worries you and what you would like from the visit. Before she starts, she tells you what she is about to do, and she pauses whenever you ask.

She has a special interest in looking after nervous patients and in children's first visits. She likes to keep dental care simple and preventive, with a written plan and a fee that you have agreed to first.

At home, Elena makes pasta by hand with her two kids and restores old bicycles in the garage. She speaks English and Italian, and is happy to chat in either.
