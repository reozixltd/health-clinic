Priya starts every visit by listening. She asks what a normal day looks like, what has changed and what you would like to get back to. Then she explains what she finds in plain words before she suggests anything.

She has a special interest in persistent back, neck and shoulder pain, and in people who spend long hours at a desk. Her plans are usually short and practical, with a few exercises that fit into a busy day and a clear idea of what to do if things flare up.

Outside the clinic, Priya grows chillies on her balcony and cooks her family's dal recipes on Sundays. Most mornings she walks the same creek path, rain or not.
