Lucas works with adults and young people, and he takes care to explain how sessions will go before they begin. He is calm and direct, and he likes to check that each plan makes sense to you.

He has a special interest in sleep and persistent pain, and in how the two affect each other. His approach is practical, with small steps that fit into daily life rather than big changes that are hard to keep up.

Lucas speaks English and German. Away from work he hikes on weekends and builds detailed model railways, which he says is a good way to switch off. He is not taking new patients right now.
