Sam takes his time. He checks your gums and teeth, cleans away build-up and tells you what he is doing as he goes. If you feel nervous, you can set the pace or ask for a break by raising your hand.

He has a special interest in gum care and in helping people find brushing and flossing habits that suit their own mouths. He tends to show rather than lecture, and he will happily go over a technique more than once.

Away from work, Sam plays double bass in a community orchestra and bakes sourdough that is better than he admits. He swims most mornings before the clinic opens.
