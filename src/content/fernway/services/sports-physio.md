A sports injury can feel like losing a part of your week, or your whole weekend. Tom works with runners, cyclists and team players, and he looks at the whole picture, including your training, your gear and your goals.

Your plan is built in stages, so you know what to do now and what comes next. Many people use our rehab gym as part of their program, with Tom close by to adjust things as you go. You will always know the reason behind each exercise.

Fees are in [our fees list](/fees/), and when you are ready you can [book an appointment](/book/) or read [what to expect at your first visit](/new-patients/).
