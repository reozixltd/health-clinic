Some things are easier to work through with someone else in the room. Our psychologists offer talking therapy in a calm and private space, with no pressure to say more than you want to. Many people find that simply having a regular, private hour to think things through makes a difference.

Sessions are unhurried and you set the pace. You decide how often to meet, and you can stop whenever you choose. Your psychologist will explain how privacy works at the start of your first session.

Fees are in [our fees list](/fees/), and when you are ready you can [book an appointment](/book/) or read [what to expect at your first visit](/new-patients/).
