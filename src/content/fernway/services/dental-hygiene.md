A good clean can make your mouth feel fresher and help you look after your gums. Sam is an oral health therapist who takes his time, tells you what he is doing and goes at your pace.

You will also get simple, friendly tips for brushing and flossing. They are tailored to your mouth, and there is no lecture. If your gums need a closer look, we will explain why and what comes next.

Fees are in [our fees list](/fees/), and when you are ready you can [book an appointment](/book/) or read [what to expect at your first visit](/new-patients/).
