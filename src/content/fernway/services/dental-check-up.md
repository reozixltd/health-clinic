A check-up should feel calm and clear, not rushed. Dr Marsh looks after adults and children, and she explains what she sees in plain words so that nothing comes as a surprise.

If dental visits make you nervous, you are in good company. Tell us when you book, and your visit will go at your pace, with a break whenever you ask. You will also leave with a clear plan and a fee, agreed before any treatment starts.

Fees are in [our fees list](/fees/), and when you are ready you can [book an appointment](/book/) or read [what to expect at your first visit](/new-patients/).
