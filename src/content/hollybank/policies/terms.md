## Using this website

This website helps you ask for an appointment and find our details. It does not give veterinary advice. If your pet is in danger, call us or our [out of hours service](/out-of-hours/).

## Cancelling or moving a visit

Please tell us at least one day before. We may charge a small fee if you miss a visit without telling us.

## Prices

Our [price list](/prices/) shows our usual prices, including VAT. We give a written [estimate](/estimates/) before treatment that costs more than 500 pounds.
