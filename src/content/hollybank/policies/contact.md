This is sample text for a demonstration site.

## Getting here

We are a five minute walk from the bus stop on Example Road. There is free parking behind the practice, with two spaces kept for visits with large dogs.

## Getting in

There is a step-free entrance and a wide door for carriers and pushchairs. Assistance dogs are welcome. Tell us when you book if your pet is anxious and we will find a quiet time. Read our [accessibility statement](/accessibility/) for more.

## After hours

When we are closed, call our out of hours service and a vet will call you back. See our [out of hours page](/out-of-hours/) for the number and what to expect.
