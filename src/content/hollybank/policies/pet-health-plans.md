## What a plan is

A pet health plan spreads the cost of routine care over the year. You pay a small amount each month.

## What it covers

A typical plan covers yearly vaccinations, flea and worm treatment, and a yearly health check. It also takes a part off the cost of other visits, like a [dental check](/services/dental/).
