## What we collect

When you ask for an appointment we collect your name, phone, email, your pet's name and type of animal, and a time that suits you. We do not ask about your pet's health on this website. You tell the vet at your visit.

## How we use it

We use your details to reply to you, to confirm and remind you about visits, and to keep our records. You can ask to see or fix your details at any time. Use the [contact page](/contact/).

## UK law

We follow UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. We send texts and emails under PECR. We only send marketing if you agree, and every message tells you how to stop.
