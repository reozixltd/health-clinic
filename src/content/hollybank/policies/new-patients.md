## How your first visit works

1. Ask for an appointment on our [booking page](/book/) or call us. Tell us the service you would like and a time that suits you.
2. We reply within 1 working day to confirm your time.
3. Bring what you need on the day, listed below.
4. After the visit we agree a plan with you and book any follow-up.

## What to bring

Bring your pet in a carrier or on a lead. Bring any vaccination card and a list of any medicines your pet takes. Bring a fresh sample if we asked for one.

## Registering

We register your pet at your first visit. You can give us details when you arrive. We do not ask about your pet's health on this website.

## Arriving

Please arrive five minutes early. See the [contact page](/contact/) for parking and how to get in.
